## Claim boundaries

Playwright engine runs are not branded Safari or physical-device evidence.
Automated accessibility scans are not VoiceOver or NVDA evidence. Live-provider
and deployed Cloudflare claims require their own dated operator run. The pages
above retain every recorded limitation and failed or superseded attempt.
